#MagicalLawsAlrathion

THE PROBLEM IS YOU HAVEN'T MADE ENOUGH SPELLS YOURSELF. YOU MADE THE FLAVOR BUT ARE RELYING TOO MUCH ON THE SYSTEM
## What is it?
### Magik
Magik is the psionic manipulation of Vraul's waveforms. In combination, these forms produce harmony or dissonance, creating Magik of different types. This also produces other effects, based on the mind of the caster and the spell cast. These effects are grounded by the strength of the spell being cast as well, have 3 stages each. Psionic energy cannot directly affect unbound (having a mind) things \[Natural Immunity to Mind Control, Charming, etc.\].

Magic is similar to Vancian casting (Stored formulae that are released from the mind when cast) in that the formulae are stored in your memory, but they or not "gone"
## Terminology 
- Interactor: Any object that lacks the ability to create its own Vraul.
- Note: Basic waveform of Vraul (Heat, Light, etc.)
- Flourish: Modifiers (the spell description) (In universe, these are things added to the design of Laments)
- Amplitude: Strength of a spell
- Carrier: A semi-stabilized field of Vraul created specifically to hold a spell.
- Projection: Projection is the process of taking requiems from inside the recesses of your mind and putting them into the world.
- Aria: Secondary effects formed by the caster
- Lament: Complex waveform (Fire, Water, etc.)
- Requiem (Spells): Lament(s) + Flourish(es)
- Virtuoso: A person who can shape Vraul.
- Muse: A person sensitive to Vraul. (Vraul can be sensed but not discerned \[Without practice\])
- Umbra(e): Blob-like parasites that can directly channel waveforms
### Notes
- Spell Class is decided by the user's energy manipulation. A user lets Vraul disperse into the air (Area), or concentrates it (Missile).
- Interference limits the stability of a given spell (How much Vraul can be put into it), which can prevent a mage from casting.
- Transmutation will not be explained (Medium-Hard system)
- Awakening of a Virtuoso can happen due to exposure + natural disposition, or sufficiently powerful artifacts.
- Vraul absorbing waveforms do exist. This is how counter-spells work.
- ***Magic is music, and music is engineering.***
## Spell Types
There are two types of spells, harmonic and dissonant. They each stabilize or destabilize other interactors. 
### Harmonic Spells
- Enhancement
- Healing
- Communicating
- Protection / Warning
- Creation / Manipulation
### Dissonant Spells
- Harming
- Illusion
- Weakening
- Destruction

# Notes

Flourishes:
- Duration
- Cost
- Prerequisite (Spells build on previous ones.)
To cast a spell, first the caster visualizes the glyph(s) in their mind, project them into the world, then run Vraul through them. In this way, they are similar to how Magic circles or circuits work.

### Grimoires
As Vraul can be used to imbue something, mages can have a grimoire. To do this, they must encode a spell with their own understanding of it. To use someone else's encoded spell, they must spend time understanding it or they just memorize and cast.

#### Basics of the System
- What purpose does Magik serve in my story? 
    A great temptation, for those with power. A strength, for those who are weak. A hope, for those hopeless.
- How hard do I want the system to be?
    Medium-Hard, with representations of mathematics, physics, and more involved (If possible). I think this will be engaging for me as a learner, as well as in a setting like this.
- How does Magik look, sound, feel, taste, and smell in your world?
    Magik looks like an ethereal force. It has sound based on the caster's Aria. Magik feels like moving through tar when it is touched. It provides little resistance itself, but the perception of the thing or person touching it feels a mental resistance. Casting Magik feels like a artist drawing on a canvas, a brilliant expression of your will and mind. Magik has no smell.
- What existing Magik systems are you drawing inspiration from? (e.g., books, movies, TV shows, fairy tales, video games)
    GorillaOfDestiny's Arcane Engineering (Conceptually, I haven't read it at all), Way of Kings (Glyph)
- What makes your Magik system stand out from those other Magik systems?
    Magik is a fundamentally psychic endeavor, which I think opens up for some interesting dynamics between casters, as stability of the mind are extremely important.

#### The Origins of Magik
- Where did Magik originally come from? (e.g., a gift from the gods, a scientific discovery, a genetic mutation)
    Magik is scientific. It is borne of human consciousness, though the capacity for Magik-based phenomena is not directly tied to psychic ability. 
- Does Magik have a specific fuel source that keeps it going?
    Magik runs off Vraul, which is a resource created by living things. It is the connection between the mind and the body.
- Where did Vraul come from?
    Vraul stems from the people who lived in the universe before its heat death and eventual compression. It is as we would call it "spirit".

#### The Costs and Limitations of Magik
- Does Magik have any negative effects on Magik users? (e.g., physical pain, faster aging, loss of sanity)
    It produces mental strain over time. A caster feels a pulling on their mind, as if something is being taken.
- Does Magik have any negative effects on the rest of the world? (e.g., climate change, pollution, chaos)
    It does not cause any negative effects inherently.
- Is Magik difficult to use? (e.g., it’s hard to learn, it requires tremendous concentration, it costs a lot of money)
    It is not exceptionally difficult, unless working in theory. Thankfully, it does not technically cost much money, though it would be commoditized if it was able to be used on the large scale.
- Does Magik have a fatal flaw that allows it to be defeated?
    If you can inhibit the mental functions of a caster, it is possible to completely prevent them from casting, even if they can visualize a spell. This feels torturous for those deeply connected to Magik.

#### How Magik Interacts with Society
- Can everyone use Magik, or does only a specific group of people use it?
    Only a specific group of people can use Magik. 
- How does Magik intersect with race, class, and power?
    Aimasturgy is reserved for the upper class, but Magik in general is new, and has not been restricted to a class, race, or power.
- How is Magik treated by the law? (e.g., Magik is a crime, Virtuosos write the laws)
    There is no basis for Magik under the law as of now.
- How has Magik affected the way society works? (e.g., government, economy, art)
    Aimasturgy has been decided the dynamics of power from the moment it could kill efficiently.
- Does society treat Magik-users and non-Magik-users differently?
    Absolutely. Magik users are treated with reverence, as though they are noble and righteous. This actually spawned a religion.
- What cultural beliefs have formed around Magik? (e.g., myth, holidays, traditions)
    The religion of the Yorda family. Myth-wise, not many, but there are days of celebrations for the innovations they bring.

# Questions about the System

#### What is your Magic called?
    Composing
#### Level of Magic System?
Ultra High - Cosmic Levels of manipulation, most usage is Medium (Kingdom built around it)
#### Learned, Inherent, or Inherent-Learned
Inherent-learned, you need to be special ***and*** learn it.

#### If inherent, how common to be born with it?
Hereditary (25% Dominant, 75% Recessive)
1 in every 10000 people are born with the capability to ***use*** magik.
1 in every 1000 people are born with the capability to detect magik.

#### What actuates the effect?
Mana, but most of the utilization is through Science Fiction (Spells are formulated through a magic circle, magic circles are similar to engineering)
### Mana - Sub-questions
- How do you control it? Why does that work?
    You don't control Vraul directly, you utilize psionic abilities/physical constructs to create a flow of mana that causes an effect. It works because Vraul is naturally directed by almost everything. Vraul is directed even by inanimate things, but the flow is not smooth enough to align with any effectual wavelengths. 
- Can you see it? Feel it? Manipulate it by hand?
    Virtuosos can see , sense , and manipulate the flow of it (through psionics) or act as a bodily conduit for other Vraul (Giving it a path). As for manipulation by hand, it would need tools/psionic abilities, or else it'll just fall through your hand.
- Are there more than one type of Vraul?
    - No, there is not. The only differences are in its form.
- Despite where you find it now, where did it come from originally?
    - It had no conception, at least not in the traditional sense. It was birthed from the natural machinations of those that came before. Those people created great technology but wiped out their own people through (breaking through the "Great Barrier"?) (Prequel book with Silas)
