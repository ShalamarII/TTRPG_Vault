## What is it?
### Magic
Magic is the psionic manipulation of Vraul's waveforms. In combination, these forms produce harmony or dissonance, creating magic of different types. This also produces other effects, based on the mind of the caster and the spell cast. These effects are grounded by the strength of the spell being cast as well, have 3 stages each. Psionic energy cannot directly affect unbound (having a mind) things \[Natural Immunity to Mind Control, Charming, etc.\].
### Vraul
Vraul is the animate's lifeblood. Magic is a matter of your power over it.
There are three core aspects of any creation; mind, body, and spirit. Vraul is the link between a spirit, the mind, and the body.
## Where Does it Come from?
Magic first originates as the fabric of the universe, Vraul. When the universe was, Everything was originally Vraul. Then, as time passed, Vraul began to refract
## Who can Cast it?
Muses are people with a natural sensitivity towards Vraul. Those of them that are exceptional are known as Virtuoso's. A Virtuoso has the Psionic capacity to reach out and touch what the Muse cannot.
# Terminology
Note: Basic Waveform (Heat, Light, etc.)
Flourish: Modifiers that apply to spells (AKA the spell description)
Amplitude: Strength of a spell
Carrier: Psionic Energy
Aria: Secondary effects formed by the caster
Lament: Complex Waveform (Fire, Water, etc.)
Requiem: Lament(s) + Flourish(es) AKA Spell
Virtuoso: A Caster of Magic
Muse: A person sensitive to Vraul.
Umbra(e): Blobby guys that can directly channel waveforms 
### Notes
- Spell Class is decided by the user's energy manipulation. A user lets magic disperse into the air (Area), or concentrates it (Missile).
- Interference limits the stability of a given spell (How much Vraul can be put into it), which can prevent a mage from casting.
- Transmutation will not be explained (Medium-Hard system)
- Awakening of a Virtuoso can happen due to exposure + natural disposition, or sufficiently powerful artifacts.
- Vraul absorbing waveforms do exist. This is how counter-spells work.

# Spell Creation
Magic utilizes the spells as powers framework.
### Spell Creation Notes
- Missile spells have a default Max range of 80 yards, though the GM should feel free to adjust range up or down if a spell’s concept demands a different range or if the spell’s effectiveness needs to be adjusted.
- Spells that change or control living, aware beings should usually be Resisted.
- Powerful spells should usually have a long list of prerequisites, be Very Hard spells, or both.
- Most spells do not have TL modifiers, but Technological spells may.
- You have a skill level, Vraul cost, and FP Cost (is a default of 1). Everything else is a modifier to those. Glyphs apply modifiers to the Skill Level, The effect of a spell determines the base cost, and FP cost is naturally 1. Creation of a spell uses the Powers as Magic system, where player rolls utilize the Skills as Magic system.

- Glyphs = Modifiers to Effective Skill Level
- Spell Effect = Base Cost
- FP Cost = 1 per level by default, can cost more.

## Player Created Spells
Use the New Inventions rules on p. B473 to devise a new spell (When a character is designing it). The required skill for designing new spells is Thaumatology (p. B225).

The process begins when the researcher describes the spell he wishes to develop. The GM then writes the spell up in the standard format, including class, college, energy cost, casting time, duration, and prerequisites. He may decide that the intended spell has prerequisites, which must be learned or invented before research on the intended spell can even begin.

The Concept roll is made against Thaumatology, instead of a Complexity modifier; subtract the prerequisite count for the new spell (p. 6). Apply a -5 penalty for any spell in a college in which the researcher knows no spells. Apply a -5 penalty in a low-mana zone.

Once the Concept phase has produced a viable incantation, the researcher (or an assistant) must invest a character point in learning the new spell. At that point, a prototype may be developed. A magical workshop is required, with accoutrements that cost $(prerequisite count +1)\*(400 energy)\*(the setting’s prevailing price per energy point (pp. 21-22)).

The researcher and his assistants may choose to offset up to 90% of this investment by enchanting the supplies themselves; 10% of the cost is consumable supplies which must be purchased.

Once built, a magical workshop may also be used for future research – the non-consumable portion of the workshop’s cost may be reused indefinitely (though critical failures may damage or destroy some of that equipment).

The Prototype roll is a roll against the new spell itself, cast ceremonially (this can be done alone; the process is similar to enchantment). The normal Prototype bonuses for qualified assistants apply, though the ceremonial magic penalties for assistants typically offset them. A critical failure on the Prototype roll is like any other critical failure on a spell; roll on the Critical Spell Failure table (p. 7). Each attempt takes a full day.
 
A successful Prototype spell may have bugs, but they are rarely tested. A major bug is indistinguishable from a normal critical failure, so bugs are typically left to surface at some later date. Established spells have had any bugs worked out by generations of wizards, but new spells may be unexpected trouble waiting to happen.

The process used to enchant a new spell into an item is a separate invention from the spell itself. An enchantment process is actually even harder than the original spell, because the enchantment also has Enchant as a prerequisite, increasing its effective prerequisite count by 13! Experimental enchanters are a dogged bunch.

The GM may allow mage Gadgeteers to apply Gadgeteering to spell invention. However, such a combination is likely to lead to a number of wild thaumaturgical innovations that not every GM will be happy to handle.

Flourishes - Modifiers
Flourishes describe the modifications to the runes (or glyphs). They might be things like the range, damage, effects, cast time, spell type, duration, etc. They also hold the glyph or glyphs together. To cast a spell, first the caster visualizes the glyph(s) in their mind, project them into the world, then run Vraul through them. In this way, they are similar to how magic circles or circuits work.

Projection: Projection is the process of taking spellforms from inside the recesses of your mind and putting them into the world.

### Grimoires
As Vraul can be used to imbue something, mages can have a grimoire. To do this, they must encode a spell with their own understanding of it. To use someone else's encoded spell, they must roll a Thaumatology or appropriate realm roll. The result is based on the following table:

| Margin of Success | Description                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1                 | You can successfully decipher the spell. You may cast it one time with a -2 penalt                                                                                                                  |
| 2-3               | You decipher the spell.You may cast it one time with no penal                                                                                                                                       |
| 4+                | You decipher and make the spell your own. You may inscribe the spell into your grimoire and cast it at a later date (Which takes 1d days) or you may cast it without a penalty to roll for 1d days. |
